Your Mission

This week, your task is to design a blueprint for a high-performing, AI-augmented team. You will define the team structure, select the right AI tools, establish collaboration rules, and create a plan to ensure everyone is equipped for success.

Task 1: Design the Team Structure

Go beyond traditional roles. Define how AI integrates into the team's workflow—not as a separate tool, but as a core collaborator. Select an initiative from the running case or propose your own to frame your design.

Task 3: Create Collaboration Protocols

Define the "rules of the road" for working with AI. How does the team ensure quality, maintain accountability, and use AI ethically?

Principle of Verification

All AI-generated output (code, text, data) must be reviewed and verified by a human expert before being finalized.

Protocol for Attribution

Team members must indicate when AI was used as a significant contributor to a piece of work (e.g., via a tag or comment).

Guideline for Data Privacy

No proprietary or personally identifiable information (PII) should be entered into public AI models.

Human-in-the-Loop for Decisions

AI can inform and provide options, but final strategic decisions must be made by human team members.

Task 4: Develop a Training Plan

Outline a training program to upskill your team. What do they need to learn to be effective and responsible users of the new AI tools?

1

Foundational AI Literacy

Basic concepts of AI/ML, understanding limitations and biases, and principles of ethical AI use.

2

Tool-Specific Workshops

Hands-on training for the specific AI tools selected for each role (e.g., a GitHub Copilot masterclass for developers).

3

Advanced Prompt Engineering

Training on how to write effective prompts to get the best possible output from generative AI tools.

Final Deliverable

Submit a comprehensive document or presentation that details your complete blueprint for the AI-augmented team, covering all four assignment tasks.
