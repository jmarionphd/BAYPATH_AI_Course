Module 4 Group Assignment

The Augmented Team Simulation

Use ← and → arrow keys or the on-screen buttons to navigate.

Your Mission

This is a practical, hands-on assignment. Your group will simulate a team meeting, actively using AI tools to collaborate on a challenge. The goal is to experience the dynamics of an augmented team firsthand and document what you learn to create a formal team charter.

Your Five Core Tasks

1. Role-Play a Meeting

Assign roles from your individual assignment and conduct a recorded meeting to solve a specific problem. Produce a brief report on your decision.

2. Use AI Tools Live

During the meeting, you must actively use AI tools (e.g., Mural AI, Copilot, Notion AI) to help with brainstorming, summarizing, or creating content.

3. Navigate Decisions

Observe and discuss the process. When does the team accept an AI suggestion? When is it debated or rejected? How is the final call made?

4. Document Lessons

Hold a debrief after the simulation to reflect on the experience. What worked? What was challenging? Document your key lessons learned.

5. Create a Team Charter

Based on your lessons, create a formal charter that defines the rules, roles, and protocols for your augmented team going forward.

The Augmented Team Charter

This is the most important deliverable. It's your team's constitution for working with AI. It should be a practical guide that any new member could read to understand how you operate.

Key Sections to Include:

  • Mission: What is your team's purpose?
  • Roles & Responsibilities: Who is accountable for what (including AI's role)?
  • AI Usage Protocols: Which tools are used for which tasks?
  • Decision-Making: How are human/AI conflicts resolved? Who makes the final call?
  • Ethics & Verification: How do you ensure AI outputs are accurate, unbiased, and properly attributed?

Submission Checklist

Combine your work into a single submission that includes the following three components:

  • 1. Meeting Report: A brief summary of your role-play session and the final decision reached.
  • 2. Lessons Learned: A bulleted list or short document detailing your reflections from the debrief.
  • 3. Augmented Team Charter: Your final, formal document outlining your team's protocols.
