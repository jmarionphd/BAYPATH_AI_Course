Group Assignment Goal & Deliverables

Your group's objective is to act as a consulting team tasked with building a comprehensive AI governance and ethics framework for a fictional company, "TransformCo."

  • Create TransformCo Governance Structure: Define who oversees AI ethics.
  • Develop Ethics Review Process: Design the project review workflow.
  • Address Specific Ethical Dilemmas: Apply your framework to hard cases.
  • Build a Regulatory Roadmap: Plan for future legal compliance.
  • Present to the Ethics Committee: Synthesize and defend your proposal.

1. Create TransformCo Governance Structure

This is the "who" of your framework. You need to design the central oversight body responsible for guiding and governing AI ethics at TransformCo.

Elaboration:

Define the committee's official name (e.g., "AI Governance Council"). Detail its charter, its authority within the company, and the roles of its members. Specify who should be on the committee (e.g., Chief Ethics Officer, VP of Engineering, General Counsel, a senior data scientist, an external advisor).

Deliverable: A formal charter document for the AI Governance Council. This should include its mission statement, member roles and responsibilities, and operational procedures (e.g., "The council will meet quarterly to review high-risk projects and annually to update AI ethics guidelines.").

2. Develop Ethics Review Process

This is the "how" of your framework. You will design the end-to-end process that project teams must follow to get their AI initiatives reviewed and approved.

Elaboration:

Create a clear, step-by-step workflow. This should start with a project intake or risk assessment form. Based on the risk level, the project would follow a specific review path (e.g., low-risk projects get automated checks, high-risk projects go to the full Governance Council). Define the possible outcomes (Approved, Approved with Conditions, Reject).

Deliverable: A process flowchart or a detailed step-by-step document illustrating the ethics review process. It should clearly show the decision points, stakeholders involved at each stage, and required documentation (like the checklist from the individual assignment).

3. Address Specific Ethical Dilemmas

This task tests the robustness of your framework. You will apply your newly designed governance structure and review process to analyze complex, real-world ethical scenarios.

Elaboration:

For each provided dilemma, your group will act as the AI Governance Council. You must walk through your own review process to assess the case. Your analysis should weigh the potential benefits against the ethical risks, referencing your AI ethics principles.

Deliverable: A written memorandum for each dilemma. Each memo should:

  1. Briefly summarize the ethical problem.
  2. Analyze the pros and cons through the lens of your framework.
  3. State your council's final decision (e.g., "Proceed with mandatory safeguards X and Y," or "Halt the project as it violates our core principle of fairness.").

4. Build a Regulatory Roadmap

This is the forward-looking part of your strategy. You need to help TransformCo prepare for the evolving landscape of AI laws and regulations.

Elaboration:

Identify 2-3 significant, near-future AI regulations or standards that are not yet law but are likely to impact TransformCo (e.g., a proposed national AI Act, new industry standards for transparency). Create a high-level plan for how the company can prepare to comply.

Deliverable: A 12-to-18-month roadmap (can be a visual timeline or a table). For each upcoming regulation, list key preparatory actions and assign them to quarters.
Example Item: "Q2 2026: Task legal and engineering teams with conducting a gap analysis of our current practices against the draft 'Safe AI Act'."

5. Present to the Ethics Committee

This is the final deliverable where you synthesize all your work into a persuasive executive presentation for the leadership of TransformCo.

Elaboration:

Your presentation should tell a coherent story. Start with the "why" (the need for governance), introduce your solution (the structure and process), show it in action (the dilemma analysis), and outline the path forward (the roadmap). Be prepared to justify your decisions.

Deliverable: A professional presentation deck (e.g., 10-12 slides). The presentation should be clear, concise, and aimed at a senior leadership audience. It serves as the executive summary of your entire project.
