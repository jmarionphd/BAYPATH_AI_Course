1 Creating Your Account

Sign-Up Options

📧 Email Sign-Up

  1. Go to miro.com/signup
  2. Enter your name
  3. Enter your work email
  4. Create a password
  5. Accept Terms & Privacy Policy
  6. Click "Get started now"

🔐 Quick Sign-Up Options

  • 🔵 Google - Sign up with Gmail
  • 🟦 Office 365 - Use Microsoft account
  • 🟪 Slack - Connect via Slack
  • ⚫ Apple ID - Sign in with Apple
  • 🔷 Facebook - Use Facebook login

⚡ Quick sign-up options are faster and don't require creating a new password!

2 Email Verification

Verification Process

📧

Check your inbox for confirmation email

→

🔢

Find your confirmation code

→

✅

Enter code or click "Confirm your email"

Important Notes

  • You have 7 days to confirm your email
  • Check your spam folder if you don't see the email
  • You can click the button in the email OR enter the code manually
  • Unconfirmed profiles are automatically deleted after 7 days

🔍 Can't find the email? Check your spam/junk folder or request a new confirmation code

3 Creating or Joining a Team

A team in Miro is your workspace where you create, store, and share boards with collaborators.

🆕 Create New Team

Choose this if:

  • You're starting fresh
  • You want to be the team admin
  • You're setting up for your company

You can:

  • Name your team
  • Invite collaborators immediately
  • Start with a free plan

🤝 Join Existing Team

Choose this if:

  • Your company already uses Miro
  • You received an invitation
  • You want to join a corporate team

You can:

  • Request access to teams
  • Join open teams in your domain
  • Wait for admin approval

💡 You can be a member of multiple teams and switch between them easily!

4 Setting Up Your Profile

Make it easy for teammates to recognize and connect with you!

  • Update your display name
  • Add your job title or role
  • Set your time zone for better collaboration
  • Configure notification preferences

🔐 Security Settings

Account Security

  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Review connected apps
  • Manage login sessions

Privacy Settings

  • Control who can find you
  • Manage email notifications
  • Set board sharing defaults

⚠️ You'll be automatically logged out after 2 weeks of inactivity for security
