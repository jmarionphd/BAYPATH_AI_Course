MONDAY

The Double Diagnosis

Delores, the new Chief Strategy Officer, arrives to find TransformCo at a critical crossroads, facing two interconnected challenges that threaten its future.

  • Innovation Crisis: Ideas are sporadic. Without formal processes, project selection is driven by intuition and internal politics, not strategy.
  • Technology Gap: Market data reveals competitors are leveraging AI, launching new products 40% faster and capturing market share.
"We're throwing darts in the dark," Delores remarked to Marcus, the head of R&D. "And our competitors have floodlights. We can't solve our innovation problem without addressing our tech deficit. They're two sides of the same coin."

WEDNESDAY

The Integration Insight

Armed with data, Marcus and Delores develop a unified approach. The solution isn't to run two separate initiatives, but to weave them together into a single, powerful strategy.

  • One Process to Rule Them All: A formal Stage-Gate process will now guide ALL initiatives, from incremental improvements to moonshot projects.
  • AI as an Enabler, Not a Project: AI tools won't be a separate silo. They will be integrated directly into the Stage-Gate process where they add the most value.
Marcus sketched on a whiteboard, connecting two diagrams. "Look," he explained to a skeptical team, "We don't just do empathy interviews *or* sentiment analysis. We do both. Our human insight guides the AI's focus, and the AI scales our understanding across millions of data points. It's a feedback loop."

This... this actually gives all of us what we need." George nodded enthusiastically. "Exactly. This isn't a compromise; it's a force multiplier."
FRIDAY

A New Vision: Project Synthesis

With universal buy-in, CEO Virginia announces "Project Synthesis" to the entire company, framing it not as a reaction to crisis, but as a proactive evolution.

  • The Mission: To build a world-class, systematic innovation capability.
  • The Tool: To strategically integrate artificial intelligence as a core enabler of that capability.
  • The Targets: Achieve 25% of revenue from new innovations and enhance 50% of our core processes with AI within three years.
In her address, Virginia was clear: "Today, we stop talking about innovation and technology as separate things. They are the left and right foot of our march forward. Project Synthesis is our commitment to learning to run."

One Integrated Solution

By addressing the process and technology crises simultaneously, TransformCo turned two weaknesses into a single, unified strength, paving the way for sustainable growth and a competitive edge.
