Your Mission

This final assignment is a capstone project focused on you. Your mission is to apply the key frameworks from this course to your own professional context. You will design a personal learning strategy, vet a new tool, improve a process, and synthesize it all into a forward-looking development plan.

Task 1: Design Your Learning Path

Design a 3-month blended learning path to develop a new capability for your current or desired role. Be specific about the mix of self-paced, social, and live learning you will use.

Example: Learning "AI for Marketing"

  • Month 1 (Self-Paced): Complete an online course on AI fundamentals.
  • Month 2 (Social): Start a small peer learning circle to discuss weekly articles on AI in marketing.
  • Month 3 (Application): Attend a live workshop on prompt engineering and apply the skills to a real marketing campaign.

Task 2: Certify an AI Tool

Select one new AI tool relevant to your role. Run it through the certification framework, assessing it on criteria like security, data privacy, and cost. Assign it a "Green," "Yellow," or "Red" status and justify your decision.

Task 3 & 4: Improve & Plan

3. Document a Process Improvement

Identify one process in your daily work (e.g., creating reports, planning projects). Document how you could use a concept or AI tool from this course to make it faster, smarter, or more efficient.

4. Create a Personal Development Plan

Synthesize everything into a 1-page plan. It should outline the skills you'll build (from your learning path), the tools you'll master (from your certification), and the impact you'll make (from your process improvement).
