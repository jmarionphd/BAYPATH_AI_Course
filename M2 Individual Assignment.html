Week 2 Individual Assignment

From Empathy to Insight

Use ← and → arrow keys or the on-screen buttons to navigate.

Assignment Goal

This week, you will gather qualitative data from both human and digital sources. Your goal is to synthesize these findings to uncover deep insights and visualize them in a customer journey map.

Your Four Core Tasks

1

Empathy Interviews

Conduct 3 one-on-one interviews to gather firsthand experiences.

2

AI Discussion Analysis

Use AI to analyze relevant online conversations (forums, social media).

3

Synthesize Findings

Combine insights from both data sources into unified themes.

4

Create Journey Map

Visualize the customer experience, overlaying it with your data.

Task 1: Empathy Interviews

  • Who: Interview 3 people (classmates, colleagues, friends, family).
  • What: Ask open-ended questions about their experiences related to your project topic.
  • How: Focus on listening, not talking. Dig deeper with "Why?" Record key quotes and observations.

Task 2: AI Discussion Analysis

  • Where: Find online communities (Reddit, Twitter, forums) discussing your topic.
  • What: Gather a sample of relevant posts and comments.
  • How: Use an AI tool (like a large language model) to summarize themes, identify common pain points, and extract key sentiments from the text data.

Task 3: Synthesize Findings

This is the most critical step. Compare your interview notes with the AI analysis. Look for:

  • Patterns: Themes that appear in both datasets.
  • Contradictions: Where do interviews and online discussions differ? Why?
  • Unified Insights: Formulate 3-5 core insights that capture the essence of your combined findings.

Task 4: Create a Customer Journey Map

A customer journey map is a visual story of a person's experience. Your map should include:

  • Stages: Key phases of the experience (e.g., Awareness, Consideration, Action).
  • Actions & Thoughts: What is the user doing and thinking at each stage?
  • Data Overlay: Annotate the map with key quotes from interviews and themes from your AI analysis to support your insights.

Final Deliverable

Submit a single document containing:

  • A brief summary of your interview and AI analysis process.
  • Your 3-5 unified insights.
  • Your visual Customer Journey Map with the data overlay.
