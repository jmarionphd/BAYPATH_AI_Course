Building TransformCo's Innovation Portfolio

Use ← and → arrow keys or the on-screen buttons to navigate.

Your Mission

Your group will act as TransformCo's innovation leadership. Your mission is to develop a complete innovation portfolio, design AI-enhanced processes to manage it, create an integrated roadmap, and present your strategy for board approval.

Your Five Core Tasks

1. Develop Portfolio

Develop a complete, balanced innovation portfolio for TransformCo using a recognized framework (e.g., 70-20-10).

2. Show Processes

Detail the innovation process (e.g., Stage-Gate) for each type of initiative in your portfolio.

3. Identify AI Enhancements

Pinpoint specific opportunities to integrate AI into your innovation processes to boost efficiency and insight.

4. Create Roadmap

Build an integrated, multi-year roadmap that visualizes the rollout of your portfolio initiatives.

5. Present for Approval

Synthesize all findings into a compelling presentation for the TransformCo "board" that clearly states your strategy, resource needs, and expected outcomes.

Board Presentation Checklist

Your final presentation should be a persuasive, data-driven strategic proposal covering:

  • 1. Portfolio Overview: A clear, balanced list of innovation initiatives.
  • 2. AI-Enhanced Processes: Visual maps of how each initiative type will be managed.
  • 3. Strategic Roadmap: A multi-year timeline with key milestones and dependencies.
  • 4. The "Ask": A clear request for resources (budget, headcount) required for success.
