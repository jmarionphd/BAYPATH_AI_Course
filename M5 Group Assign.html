The Go/No-Go Decision

Your Mission

This is the final presentation to the "board." Your group will select one innovation initiative for TransformCo, build an AI-accelerated Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to test its core hypothesis, and present a data-backed recommendation to either proceed with further investment (Go) or to pivot/cancel the project (No-Go).

Your Five Core Tasks

1. Develop MVP

Define and build the smallest possible product that allows you to test your riskiest assumption for a TransformCo innovation.

2. Use AI to Accelerate

Actively use AI tools (e.g., Copilot for code, Midjourney for visuals, ChatGPT for content) and document how they sped up your MVP development.

3. Conduct Validation

Test your MVP with a target audience. Collect both quantitative (e.g., clicks, time on task) and qualitative (e.g., interview feedback) data.

4. Present Findings

Create a concise presentation that clearly shows your results using data visualizations (charts, graphs, key quotes).

5. Recommend Go/No-Go

Based on your evidence, make a clear, decisive recommendation to the "board." Justify your decision with the data you collected.

Making the Final Recommendation

Your final recommendation is a business decision based on evidence, not opinion. Use your findings to justify your choice.

Go

Proceed with further investment. This is justified if:

  • Your hypothesis was validated.
  • Key metrics met or exceeded targets.
  • Qualitative feedback was strongly positive.
  • You have a clear next step for the product.

No-Go

Pivot or cancel the project. This is justified if:

  • Your hypothesis was invalidated.
  • Users were indifferent or confused.
  • The results show no product-market fit.
  • The problem is not worth solving.

Final Presentation to the Board

Your group will deliver a concise, executive-level presentation that acts as your final deliverable. It must include:

  • 1. The MVP: A clear description and demo/screenshot of the product you tested.
  • 2. The Findings: Key data visualizations and qualitative insights from your validation.
  • 3. The Recommendation: A final, unambiguous "Go" or "No-Go" decision.
  • 4. The Evidence: A summary of the data that directly supports your recommendation.
