MONDAY

The Town Hall

With rumors and anxiety swirling, Delores and CEO Virginia host an all-hands meeting to address the "elephant in the room" head-on.

  • A public commitment: No layoffs due to AI for the next two years.
  • Announcement of a $5 million reskilling budget to train the existing workforce.
  • Launch of an "AI Ambassador" program to create internal champions.
Virginia looked out at the packed auditorium. "Let me be clear. We don't believe AI is here to replace you. But the market is changing, and people who know how to use AI will replace people who don't. Our goal is to make sure every single one of you becomes the person who knows how to use AI."
TUESDAY

The Union Negotiation

Baldemar and Delores meet with skeptical union representatives, who fear AI is a Trojan horse for job elimination.

  • The meeting starts with hostility and accusations.
  • Delores pivots by demonstrating a predictive maintenance tool, showing how it alerts a worker to a problem instead of automating their job.
  • The Proposal: A new "AI-Assisted" pay grade, offering premium wages for workers who complete training and use the new tools.
The lead union rep crossed his arms. "This is just a fancy way to cut jobs." Baldemar stepped in. "No. This is a way to save jobs. Our competitors are automating. We want to supercharge our people. Help us design a pilot program. You tell us what fair looks like." The tension broke, and the conversation began.
WEDNESDAY

The War for Talent

TransformCo can't outbid big tech for scarce AI experts. Delores implements a creative, multi-pronged strategy to build and attract talent differently.

  • Build: An internal "AI Academy" is launched to upskill 500 high-potential employees in the first year.
  • Borrow: A new partnership with a local university creates an AI residency program for graduate students.
  • Bring Back: A "Boomerang" program actively recruits former employees who have gained AI skills elsewhere.
  • Buy: In a strategic move, they acquire a 12-person AI startup, gaining both unique technology and a cohesive, expert team.
THURSDAY

The Culture Catalyst

To make AI part of everyone's job, the company launches "Innovation Friday," dedicating time and resources to grassroots experimentation.

  • Every employee is given four hours per week for AI-related learning and projects.
  • AI tool licenses are provided to everyone who completes a basic training course.
  • An internal "Shark Tank" is created, where employees can pitch their AI-powered ideas for funding.
The first "Shark Tank" winner wasn't an executive, but a warehouse worker. Her idea: an AI-powered inventory assistant using a phone's camera to identify, count, and log pallets instantly. She received a $50,000 budget and a team to build it, sending a powerful message throughout the company.
FRIDAY

The Superagency Emerges

The cultural and technical investments begin paying off as employees start augmenting their roles with AI, achieving remarkable results.

  • A sales rep uses an AI assistant to analyze customer data, tripling her number of closed deals.
  • A plant manager uses a predictive model to prevent a catastrophic equipment failure, saving an estimated $2 million.
  • A junior marketing coordinator uses generative AI to create a social media campaign that outperforms the work of their expensive external agency.
  • A customer service rep uses an AI knowledge base to resolve complex issues in minutes, not hours, dramatically improving satisfaction.

Human-Centered Transformation

TransformCo's greatest success wasn't in deploying algorithms, but in unleashing the augmented intelligence of its entire workforce. By focusing on people first, they built a culture of innovation that technology alone could never create.
