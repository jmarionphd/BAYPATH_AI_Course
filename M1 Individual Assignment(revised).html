PDMA Best Practices Framework

A comprehensive framework for assessing innovation and product development maturity

AI Maturity Framework Selection

You must choose ONE AI maturity framework for your assessment

🔷 Microsoft AI Maturity Model

5 Stages: Foundational → Approaching → Aspirational → Mature → Advanced

Focus: Infrastructure, data, governance, and AI deployment capabilities

🔷 Google Cloud AI Adoption Framework

4 Phases: Tactical → Strategic → Transformational → Differentiating

Focus: Business value, scale, and competitive advantage from AI

🔷 Gartner AI Maturity Model

5 Levels: Awareness → Active → Operational → Systemic → Transformational

Focus: Organizational readiness and AI integration across enterprise

🔷 MIT CISR AI Maturity Framework

Focus: Data quality, algorithmic sophistication, and infrastructure capabilities

Critical Reflection Component

"When do innovation frameworks vs. AI tools solve problems?"

What This Question Is Asking

Innovation Frameworks

  • Structured processes (Stage-Gate, Agile)
  • Portfolio management methods
  • Innovation strategy approaches
  • Organizational design principles
  • Cultural change initiatives

AI Tools

  • Machine learning algorithms
  • Predictive analytics platforms
  • Natural language processing
  • Computer vision systems
  • Automation technologies

Key Considerations for Your Reflection:

  • What types of problems are best solved by process/structure vs. technology?
  • When is human judgment and framework discipline more valuable than AI?
  • When does AI provide capabilities that frameworks cannot?
  • How do they complement each other?

Writing Your Reflection

Academic Approach

Structure

  • Introduction: Frame the question
  • Analysis: Compare and contrast
  • Examples: Use your organization
  • Conclusion: Synthesize insights

Depth

  • Go beyond surface observations
  • Consider context and nuance
  • Acknowledge complexity
  • Draw on theory and practice

Potential Themes to Explore

  • Problem Complexity: Structured vs. unstructured problems
  • Human vs. Machine: Judgment, creativity, and pattern recognition
  • Organizational Readiness: Culture, skills, and infrastructure
  • Scale & Speed: When does volume or velocity matter?
  • Complementarity: How frameworks enable AI, and vice versa
  • Risk & Ethics: Governance considerations for each approach

Remember: There's no single "right" answer. Demonstrate critical thinking and support your arguments with evidence.

What You Need to Identify

Problems best solved through AI tools, automation, data analytics, or digital platforms

Important: These should be real or realistic scenarios from your chosen organization, not generic examples

Process-Focused Scenarios

When Process/Framework Is the Answer

Example Type 1

Alignment Problems

Innovation projects not aligned with strategy

Example Type 2

Coordination Issues

Cross-functional teams not collaborating effectively

Example Type 3

Cultural Barriers

Risk-averse culture stifling innovation

Characteristics of Process Solutions:

  • Problem involves human behavior, decisions, or collaboration
  • Solution requires organizational change or new ways of working
  • Success depends on clarity, structure, or governance
  • Technology alone cannot solve the underlying issue
  • Requires leadership, culture, or capability development

For Each Scenario, Describe:

  • The specific problem or challenge
  • Why process/framework is the appropriate solution
  • What process or framework would address it
  • Expected outcomes

Technology-Focused Scenarios

When Technology/AI Is the Answer

Example Type 1

Scale Problems

Processing thousands of customer feedback responses

Example Type 2

Pattern Recognition

Identifying market trends from big data

Example Type 3

Automation Needs

Repetitive tasks consuming valuable time

Characteristics of Technology Solutions:

  • Problem involves large-scale data processing or analysis
  • Solution requires speed, accuracy, or computational power
  • Success depends on pattern recognition or prediction
  • Human capacity is insufficient or inefficient
  • Requires automation, optimization, or augmentation

For Each Scenario, Describe:

  • The specific problem or challenge
  • Why technology/AI is the appropriate solution
  • What technology or AI tool would address it
  • Expected outcomes

The Integration Perspective

Critical Insight

Most real-world problems require BOTH process and technology solutions working together

Process Enables Technology

  • Governance frameworks for AI ethics
  • Data quality processes for ML
  • Change management for adoption
  • Portfolio management for AI investments
  • Cross-functional collaboration for implementation

Technology Enables Process

  • AI tools for portfolio optimization
  • Analytics for process improvement
  • Automation of stage-gate reviews
  • Digital platforms for collaboration
  • Predictive models for decision support

Advanced Thinking:

While you must identify scenarios where one is "the answer," also consider in your reflection how process and technology interact and reinforce each other in practice.

Success Criteria

What Makes an Excellent Submission?

🎯 Depth of Analysis

Goes beyond surface-level observations to demonstrate genuine understanding of both frameworks and organizational realities

🎯 Evidence-Based Reasoning

Assessments and scenarios supported by specific examples, data, or observable practices from the organization

🎯 Critical Thinking

Reflection demonstrates nuanced understanding of when and why different approaches are appropriate

🎯 Integration & Synthesis

Connects insights across the three components; shows how innovation maturity and AI maturity relate

🎯 Academic Rigor

Appropriate tone, structure, and depth for graduate-level work; proper use of frameworks and terminology

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ Superficial Assessment

Simply stating maturity levels without justification or evidence

❌ Generic Scenarios

Using textbook examples instead of organization-specific situations

❌ Binary Thinking

Treating process and technology as completely separate rather than complementary

❌ Lack of Reflection Depth

Answering the reflection question with a simple list instead of thoughtful analysis

❌ Misunderstanding Frameworks

Applying frameworks incorrectly or conflating different concepts

❌ No Integration

Treating the three components as completely separate tasks
