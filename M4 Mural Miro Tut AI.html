The Synthesis Problem

Digital whiteboards are fantastic for generating ideas. But this often leads to "sticky note chaos." Manually sorting, clustering, and summarizing hundreds of ideas is time-consuming and can cause valuable insights to be missed.

How AI Transforms Whiteboarding

Cluster & Theme

Automatically group related sticky notes into themes, turning chaos into organized categories.

Summarize

Instantly generate concise summaries of entire boards, sections, or clusters of ideas.

Generate Ideas

Break through creative blocks by asking AI to generate new ideas based on existing content.

Create Content

Turn brainstorming notes into structured content like user stories, mind maps, or full presentations.

From Brainstorm to Action Plan

Prompts for Your Next Session

FOR SYNTHESIS:

"Cluster these sticky notes by common themes and give each cluster a title."

FOR BRAINSTORMING:

"Generate 10 more ideas for a new mobile app feature, based on the notes in this section."

FOR ACTION:

"Turn these selected notes into user stories with acceptance criteria."

FOR UNDERSTANDING:

"Summarize the key decisions and action items from this entire board."

Your Intelligent Partner

AI features in Mural and Miro act as a facilitator's assistant. They handle the tedious work of organizing and synthesizing, freeing up human brainpower to focus on what matters most: creativity, critical thinking, and strategic decision-making.
