Advanced Research Techniques

Focus Modes

  • Academic: Peer-reviewed papers and scholarly sources
  • Writing: Draft reports and summaries
  • Video: Product demos and conference talks
  • Reddit: Community discussions and authentic opinions

Iterative Deep Dives

  • Start with general market overview
  • Follow up with specific segments
  • Drill into particular features
  • Request comparisons
  • Ask for implications

Multi-Source Validation

Ask the same question in different ways • Request specific source types • Cross-reference data points • Verify numerical data with follow-ups

Best Practices for Effective Research

Craft Effective Queries

  • Be specific with details
  • Include context (time, region, segment)
  • Use comparative language
  • Request specific formats

Leverage Follow-Ups

  • Build on previous answers
  • Maintain conversation context
  • Explore implications

Verify Critical Information

  • Click through to sources
  • Cross-reference statistics
  • Note source dates
  • Be aware of biases

Organize Research

  • Use descriptive thread titles
  • Separate research streams
  • Export important findings
  • Maintain research log

Limitations & Considerations

⏰ Information Recency

Some data may lag for private companies or emerging markets. Always check source dates.

🎯 Data Accuracy

AI synthesis can occasionally misinterpret. Verify critical statistics by reviewing sources.

🔒 Proprietary Data Gaps

Cannot access paywalled content or confidential information. Supplement with licensed tools.

📏 Depth vs. Breadth

Excels at breadth and synthesis but may lack depth of specialized reports.

🌐 Geographic Limitations

Coverage stronger for English-language markets. Emerging markets may have limited data.

🧠 Human Judgment Required

AI augments but doesn't replace strategic thinking and domain expertise.
