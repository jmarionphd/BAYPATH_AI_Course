What is DataRobot?

DataRobot is an enterprise AI platform that automates the end-to-end process of building, deploying, and managing machine learning models. It empowers a wide range of users—from data scientists to business analysts—to get powerful insights from their data, fast.

The Challenge of Traditional ML

Building a single production-ready model is a complex, time-consuming process requiring deep expertise at every step.

Key Features for Analysis

Automated Machine Learning (AutoML)

DataRobot automatically builds and tests hundreds of models from different algorithms, ranking them on a competitive leaderboard.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Understand exactly *why* a model is making its predictions with easy-to-interpret visuals like Feature Impact and Prediction Explanations.

Automated Time Series

Build sophisticated forecasting models with just a few clicks, automatically handling trends, seasonality, and complex time-based features.

No-Code AI Apps

Turn your best-performing models into simple, interactive applications that business stakeholders can use to explore scenarios and make decisions.

Democratizing Data Science

DataRobot abstracts away the complexity of machine learning, allowing teams to focus on solving business problems. It accelerates the path to valuable insights, enabling more people across the organization to leverage the power of AI.
