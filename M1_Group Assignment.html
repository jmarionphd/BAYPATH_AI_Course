Purpose

This deck guides your group through three integrated deliverables for TransformCo:
  • Map key stakeholders on an Innovation / AI Readiness matrix.
  • Propose an integrated governance structure spanning innovation and AI.
  • Create a practical roadmap with process and technology milestones.

Deliverable 1 Stakeholders

Identify, categorize, and place stakeholders on a 2×2 readiness matrix.

Deliverable 2 Governance

Define roles, forums, and decision flows bridging innovation and AI.

Deliverable 3 Roadmap

Sequence process and technology milestones with clear owners.
Tip: Keep the three deliverables aligned—stakeholder needs should inform governance, which should constrain and enable the roadmap.
