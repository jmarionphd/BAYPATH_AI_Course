MONDAY

The Board Preview

Delores presents her draft AI strategy to CEO Virginia. The feedback is blunt and highlights a critical flaw: the strategy is a list of projects, not a coherent vision.

  • "This is a laundry list. There are too many initiatives with no clear prioritization."
  • "Where is the single, game-changing idea? The breakthrough innovation?"
  • "How does any of this build a competitive moat that our rivals can't cross in six months?"
Virginia softened her tone. "Delores, you've identified all the right ingredients. Now you need to write the recipe. We need a story that explains not just what we're doing, but why it matters and how it all connects to win the market."
TUESDAY

"Now we can see the whole picture," Marcus said, looking at the board. "We're funding the present, improving the near-future, and inventing the long-term, all at once."
WEDNESDAY

The Competitor Bombshell

The market forces their hand. A major competitor acquires an AI startup for $200M, announcing an "AI Cleaning Concierge." TransformCo's stock immediately drops 8%.

In an emergency strategy session, the debate is heated:

  • John (Sales): "We're getting calls from investors and customers. We need to respond TODAY! Announce our own AI assistant!"
  • Patricia (R&D): "A knee-jerk reaction is suicide. We can't just copy them; we'll be playing catch-up forever."
  • Delores (Strategy): "They've shown their hand. Let's not match them, let's leapfrog them. They're focused on consumers. We can win by enabling retailers with a B2B2C platform they can't replicate."
THURSDAY

Launching "Lab2025"

To accelerate breakthrough ideas, Delores gets approval to establish Lab2025, a dedicated space for rapid AI experimentation, insulated from corporate bureaucracy.

  • Protected Teams: Cross-functional teams are freed from their daily duties to focus solely on innovation.
  • Direct Access: Provides sandboxed access to the new data platform, AI tools, and resources.
  • Rapid Validation: Mandate to run 90-day sprints to validate or kill new concepts quickly and cheaply.
  • First Project: The lab's first mission is to develop a prototype for an AI sustainability advisor for B2B customers.
"This is our early warning system," Delores explained to the leadership team. "It's where we'll place our riskier bets and find the next big thing before it finds us."
FRIDAY

The Alliance Expands

In an unexpected development, the new strategic clarity inspires collaboration. Baldemar (Operations) and Patricia (R&D) jointly propose a major initiative.

The "Smart Factory Initiative":

  • Computer Vision for 100% automated quality control on production lines.
  • AI-Optimized Scheduling to reduce downtime and increase throughput.
  • Predictive Maintenance rolled out across all global manufacturing plants.
  • Estimated Value: A staggering $25M in annual savings and efficiencies by year two.
"Patricia's team has the algorithms, and my team has the machines," Baldemar stated in the meeting. "For years we've worked in separate buildings. It's time we worked on the same bottom line. This initiative connects the lab to the factory floor."

From Reaction to Revolution

Forced by internal pressure and external threats, TransformCo pivoted from a scattered collection of projects to a focused, prioritized portfolio—balancing short-term gains with long-term, game-changing innovation.
