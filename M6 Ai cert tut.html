The "Wild West" of Enterprise AI

Uncontrolled adoption of AI tools creates a major conflict: employees want to innovate, but the organization must manage risk.

Employee Drive

"I need the best tools to do my job faster and better."

Organizational Risk

"How do we protect company data, control costs, and ensure compliance?"

The Solution: A Certification Process

A certification program is a formal governance framework that vets AI tools before they are widely used. It acts as a gateway, blocking high-risk tools while approving safe and valuable ones, turning the "Wild West" into a managed innovation ecosystem.

The Certification Workflow

A Tiered Approach to Risk

Not all tools are equal. A tiered system provides clear guidance to employees on how and when approved tools can be used.

Green / Approved

Safe for general use

Tools with enterprise-grade security and a private tenancy. Safe for use with confidential company data.

Yellow / Restricted

Use with caution

Public tools that have been vetted as useful. Approved ONLY for public or non-sensitive data.

Red / Denied

Do not use

Tools with unacceptable security risks, unclear data policies, or prohibitive licensing terms.

Innovation with Guardrails

An AI tool certification program is a foundational piece of a modern governance strategy. It replaces fear with a framework, enabling employees to experiment and innovate responsibly, while protecting the organization from undue risk. It's how you balance the need for speed with the need for safety.
