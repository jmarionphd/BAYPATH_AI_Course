The Scenario & The Goal

It is one year after your initial proposal. Your group, acting as the transformation leadership team, is now presenting to the TransformCo Board of Directors. Your goal is to showcase Year 1 progress and present a compelling case for the Year 2 strategic plan and budget.

Key Presentation Pillars:

  • Demonstrate a successfully integrated portfolio.
  • Show evidence of a maturing innovation process.
  • Quantify the value created by AI initiatives.
  • Outline a clear plan for scaling and sustainability.
  • Report on key metrics and governance effectiveness.

1. Demonstrate an Integrated Portfolio

Your presentation must move beyond the theoretical "dual transformation." Show the board a tangible, integrated portfolio of initiatives across the core and new businesses.

Elaboration:

Visually represent how the core business (Transformation A) and the new venture (Transformation B) are now operating. Highlight the synergies you predicted last year and show evidence of them working. For example, show how data from the core business is fueling the new AI venture's success.

Deliverable Component: A portfolio slide in your presentation that maps key Year 1 projects. Use a framework like the Three Horizons of Growth to position initiatives and show a balanced approach to managing the present and inventing the future.

2. Show Innovation Process Maturity

Prove to the board that your innovation frameworks are not just buzzwords; they are a functioning, value-producing system.

Elaboration:

Present evidence that your innovation process is working. This includes both successes and "intelligent failures." Showcase the innovation pipeline: how many ideas were explored? How many prototypes were built? How many were killed after failing validation tests (which saves money)? This demonstrates disciplined innovation.

Deliverable Component: A section in your presentation on "The Innovation Engine." Include key metrics for your innovation pipeline (e.g., idea-to-pilot conversion rate) and briefly feature a case study of a project that went through the full process.

3. Highlight AI Value Creation

This is where you connect your AI strategy to the bottom line. The board wants to see a return on the company's significant investment in AI.

Elaboration:

Present concrete, quantifiable results from Year 1 AI initiatives. Divide this into two parts:

  • AI in the Core: Show metrics like cost savings from automation, revenue lift from personalization engines, or improved efficiency in operations.
  • AI in the New Venture: Show progress metrics like user adoption for an AI-powered product, accuracy of a new predictive model, or the creation of a valuable, proprietary dataset.

Deliverable Component: A dedicated "AI ROI" section in your presentation. Use clear charts and hard numbers to make your case. For every AI initiative mentioned, be prepared to answer "What value did this create?"

4. Address Scaling and Sustainability

Year 1 was about pilots and validation. Your Year 2 plan must focus on scaling up what works and ensuring long-term viability.

Your plan must address:

  • Scaling Plan: Which successful Year 1 pilots will be scaled in Year 2? What resources (budget, talent, technology) are required?
  • Financial Sustainability: Present a path to profitability for the new venture. When is it expected to become self-funding?
  • Talent & Capability: How will you continue to build the necessary AI and innovation skills within the organization?

Deliverable Component: The core of your "Year 2 Strategic Plan" section. This part of the presentation should clearly articulate your strategic priorities and include a specific "ask" for the resources needed to achieve them.

5. Include Metrics and Governance Updates

This is the accountability portion of your presentation. You must report on your Year 1 performance and show that your governance framework is effective.

Key elements to report:

  • Year 1 Scorecard: Present a dashboard showing Year 1 performance against the KPIs you established in your original plan. Be transparent about both hits and misses.
  • Governance in Action: Provide a brief update from the AI Governance Council. How many projects were reviewed? What were the key decisions or policy updates made in Year 1?
  • Year 2 KPIs: Propose the key metrics that the board should use to measure the success of your Year 2 plan.

Deliverable Component: A concise "Performance & Oversight" section. This builds credibility and shows the board that your team is managing the transformation in a disciplined, transparent manner.

The Final Deliverable: 45-Minute Board Presentation

Your task is to synthesize all of these components into a single, compelling, and professional 45-minute narrative.

Presentation Flow:

Intro:
Recap the Vision
⇒
Part 1:
Y1 Results & Learnings (Portfolio, AI ROI, Metrics)
⇒
Part 2:
The Y2 Strategic Plan (Scaling, Sustainability)
⇒
The Ask:
Y2 Budget & Resources
⇒
Conclusion:
Future Outlook

Remember, you are not just reporting information; you are persuading the board to continue investing in your strategic direction. Your story must be clear, data-driven, and forward-looking.
