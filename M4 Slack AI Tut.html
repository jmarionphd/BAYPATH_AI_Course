The Async Challenge

Asynchronous collaboration is powerful, but it creates long, complex conversations. Catching up after time away is difficult, and finding key decisions buried in threads is a huge time sink.

How Slack AI Transforms Async Work

Summarize Threads & Channels

Get the gist of any conversation in seconds. Catch up on a channel you've been away from without reading every message.

Search for Answers

Ask questions in natural language and get clear answers based on your company's collective knowledge in Slack.

Create Recaps

Automatically generate daily or weekly digests of key channels to stay informed without the noise.

Tutorial: Acquiring Slack AI

Slack AI is a paid add-on for eligible plans. Here’s how a Workspace Owner or Admin can add it:

  1. Navigate to Settings & administration > Manage apps.
  2. Search for and select Slack AI.
  3. Follow the prompts to purchase and add it to your workspace.

Prerequisites

  • Must be on a paid plan (Pro, Business+, or Enterprise Grid).
  • Must be a Workspace Owner or Admin.
