Assignment Goal

Your group's mission is to synthesize all individual research from Week 2. You will consolidate findings into clear problem statements, quantify the opportunities, and build a compelling business case for improvement.

Your Core Tasks

1

Define Problem Statements

Combine all individual research into clear, actionable problem statements.

2

Size Opportunities

Use AI tools to add quantitative data to your qualitative findings.

3

Build Business Case

Create a persuasive argument for improvements using all your data.

4

Present Findings

Integrate all components into a single, cohesive presentation.

Task 1: Combine & Define

As a group, review all individual empathy interviews and AI analyses. Your first step is to synthesize this collective knowledge.

  • Discuss common themes and surprising outliers.
  • Group related insights together.
  • Distill your findings into 2-3 concise problem statements.

Task 2: Size Opportunities with AI

Go beyond qualitative data. Use AI-powered research tools to add numbers to your narrative.

  • Quantify the problem: How many people are affected?
  • Market Sizing: What is the potential market size for a solution?
  • Trend Analysis: Is this problem growing or shrinking?

Task 3: Build the Business Case

A strong business case blends story with data. Combine your qualitative and quantitative findings to create a persuasive argument for why this problem needs to be solved.

  • Qualitative Support: Use powerful quotes and stories from interviews.
  • Quantitative Support: Use market data and statistics from your AI research.

Recommended Tools & Methods

Your team must use one or more of the following to complete the assignment. Be prepared to discuss why you chose your methods.

1. Empathy Interviews + AI

Use AI for transcription and initial theme analysis.

2. Journey Mapping + Analytics

Overlay journey maps with predictive analytics on user behavior.

3. Perplexity

Conduct deep, conversational market and competitor research.

4. ChatGPT

Identify patterns across large, unstructured text datasets.

5. Human Synthesis for Meaning-Making

The crucial final step of using human intuition and discussion to derive deep meaning from all data sources.

Final Group Deliverable

Submit a single group presentation that includes:

  • Your final, agreed-upon Problem Statements.
  • The quantitative data and AI-driven insights used for opportunity sizing.
  • Your complete Business Case with both qualitative and quantitative support.
  • A brief summary of the tools and methods your group used.
