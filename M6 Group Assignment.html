Module 6 Group Assignment

Leading the Change at TransformCo

Use ← and → arrow keys or the on-screen buttons to navigate.

Your Mission

As TransformCo's Change Management team, your final mission is to create a comprehensive, actionable plan to build the organization's AI capabilities. This involves not just a training plan, but a holistic strategy for communication, managing resistance, and measuring success.

Your Five Core Tasks

1. Develop Capability Strategy

Define the core AI-related skills TransformCo needs at different levels (e.g., leadership, managers, individual contributors).

2. Design Training Curriculum

Create a high-level, blended learning curriculum for one of the key capabilities you identified.

3. Address Resistance

Anticipate 2-3 specific points of resistance to AI adoption and create a plan to address each scenario.

4. Create Communication Plan

Outline a plan to communicate the "why, what, and how" of this change to the entire organization.

5. Build Measurement Framework

Define the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) you will use to measure the success of your change initiative.

Focus: Addressing Resistance

Change is hard. A great strategy anticipates and proactively manages the human element of transformation.

Example Scenario Planning

SCENARIO:

An employee fears, "AI is going to take my job."

YOUR RESPONSE PLAN:

  • Communication: Launch a campaign focused on "Augmentation, not Replacement."
  • Training: Offer learning paths on how AI can enhance their specific role.
  • Incentives: Publicly recognize and reward employees who find innovative ways to use AI in their work.

Final Presentation to the Board

Your group will deliver a final presentation that is your comprehensive Change Management Plan for TransformCo. It must include:

  • 1. The Capability Strategy: Who needs to learn what?
  • 2. The Curriculum Design: How will they learn it?
  • 3. The Resistance Plan: How will you manage pushback?
  • 4. The Communication Plan: How will you tell the story?
  • 5. The Measurement Framework: How will you measure success?
