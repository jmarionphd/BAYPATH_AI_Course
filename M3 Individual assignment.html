Assignment Goal

Your goal is to design a modern, AI-integrated Stage-Gate process for a specific innovation initiative. You will analyze AI's impact, establish decision-making criteria for its use, and build a compelling business case supported by an ROI model.

Your Four Core Tasks

1. Design Stage-Gate Process

Select an initiative (from the case or your own) and design a Stage-Gate process. Clearly identify where and how AI tools can be integrated at each stage and gate.

2. Map AI's Value Impact

For your proposed process, create a map that analyzes where AI adds tangible value (e.g., speed, accuracy) and where it might destroy value (e.g., bias, loss of human insight).

3. Create Decision Criteria

Develop a clear set of criteria a manager would use to decide whether to adopt a specific AI tool within your proposed process. Think about cost, risk, and strategic alignment.

4. Build an ROI Model

Choose one AI integration point from your design and build a simple Return on Investment (ROI) model. Estimate costs, project benefits, and calculate the potential financial return.

Submission Checklist

Ensure your submission is a cohesive report that includes the following four key deliverables:

  • 1. AI-Integrated Stage-Gate Diagram: A visual map of your process.
  • 2. Value Impact Analysis: A clear breakdown of AI's pros and cons.
  • 3. AI Adoption Criteria: A concise list or rubric for decision-making.
  • 4. ROI Model: A financial model for one AI initiative.
