PDMA Body of Knowledge

Foundations for Product Development Excellence

A practical summary of PDMA’s BoK: domains, tools, and practices to build a high-performance innovation system.

What is the PDMA BoK?

Authoritative reference for product development practice and NPDP certification

Purpose

Provide a common language, structure, and set of best practices across strategy, process, research, design, teams, and lifecycle.

Outcomes

Higher success rates, better portfolio ROI, faster time-to-market, and more predictable launches.

Eight Core Domains (PDMA BoK)

Why a ring?

Domains are interdependent—strategy informs process, research feeds design, and lifecycle data loops back.

Tailor to context

Blend phase-gate governance with agile delivery; keep evidence-based decisions at gates.

Strategy & Portfolio Management

Key Practices

  • Opportunity identification & market mapping
  • Portfolio mix, scoring models, risk balance
  • Strategic roadmaps and option bets
  • Clear value propositions and target segments

Metrics

  • NPV, IRR, Payback, R&D intensity
  • Hit rates, time-to-money, pipeline health
  • Strategic fit, technical risk, market risk

NPD Process Models

Stage-Gate, Agile, Lean, and Hybrids

Stage-Gate

Phases with evidence-based gate decisions (Go/Kill/Hold/Recycle).

Agile

Iterative sprints, backlog-driven, empirical control (great for software).

Lean

Eliminate waste, rapid learning cycles, small bets before scale.

Best of both

  • Use gates for governance; use Agile within development and validation phases.
  • Timebox experiments; promote evidence over opinions.
  • Calibrate rigor by risk and investment tier.

Voice of the Customer & Analytical Tools

Discovery

Contextual inquiry, JTBD interviews, ethnography, diary studies.

Evaluation

Concept tests, conjoint/MaxDiff, Kano, pricing research, usability.

Planning

Roadmapping, QFD/House of Quality, TRIZ, risk (FMEA), financials.

Design & Development

Core Activities

  • Requirements, architecture, specs, risk (FMEA)
  • Iterative prototypes: feasibility → usability → pilot
  • DFM/DFA, quality planning, verification/validation
  • UX research, accessibility, service design

Readiness Gates

  • Design Readiness (DR)
  • Manufacturing Readiness (MRL)
  • Launch Readiness (LR)

Teams, Culture, and Organization

Governance

Clear roles (sponsor, core team, gatekeepers), decision rights, and escalation paths.

Ways of Working

Collaboration rituals, visibility, WIP limits, and empowered cross-functional teams.

Capability

Training, communities of practice, NPDP, and onboarding playbooks.

  • Align incentives to portfolio and product outcomes, not functions alone.
  • Psychological safety drives discovery and honest gate decisions.

Launch & Lifecycle Management

From commercialization to post-launch optimization

Commercialization

  • Positioning, messaging, pricing, channels
  • Sales enablement, readiness checks, supply chain ramp
  • Regulatory & compliance controls

Lifecycle

  • KPIs: adoption, retention, NPS/CSAT, margin
  • Continuous improvement, A/B tests, PQCs
  • Line extensions, refreshes, EOL strategy

Ethics & Professionalism • Wrap-up

Ethics

Responsible research, data integrity, privacy, safety, sustainability, and transparency in claims.

Takeaways

  • Use the BoK as a capability model and common language.
  • Tailor rigor to risk; keep evidence-based gates.
  • Invest in skills: VoC, analysis, design, and governance.

Next Steps
