Your Mission

This assignment brings everything together. Your mission is to execute a full, rapid validation cycle using AI at every step. You will build a prototype, test a hypothesis, analyze the results with AI assistance, and make a final, human-led strategic judgment.

Task 1: Build a Prototype Dashboard

Using AI tools, create a high-fidelity prototype of a dashboard that tracks the progress of a new product or service. This is not about live data; it's about visualizing the ideal information a team would need.

Tools: Use text-to-image AI (e.g., Midjourney) for concepts, UI generation tools (e.g., v0.dev, Galileo AI), or even an LLM like ChatGPT to generate HTML/CSS for a static page.

Task 2: Design a Validation Experiment

Define a clear, testable hypothesis for your dashboard. Then, outline an experiment to validate it. Who will you test it with? What key metric will determine success?

Example Experiment Design

  • Hypothesis: "We believe providing this dashboard to project stakeholders will reduce the time they spend in status meetings by 25%, by giving them self-serve access to key metrics."
  • Participants: 5 Project Managers and 5 Team Leads.
  • Method: Show the prototype to participants. Ask them to find 3 specific pieces of information (e.g., user growth, main feedback). Record the time taken and gather qualitative feedback.
  • Success Metric: Average time to find all information is under 2 minutes.

Task 3 & 4: Analyze and Judge

Describe how you would use AI to analyze the (hypothetical) data from your experiment. Then, based on that AI-generated summary, make a final, human judgment call.

AI ANALYSIS:

"Use an LLM to transcribe interview recordings and summarize key themes. Prompt: 'Summarize the top 3 friction points mentioned in these user feedback transcripts.'"

HUMAN JUDGMENT:

"The AI summary shows that while users found the data, they didn't trust it. Our success metric was met, but the core value is missing. Decision: Pivot. We need to add a 'Data Sources' feature before proceeding."

Submission Checklist

Submit a single document or presentation that details your validation loop, including:

  • 1. The Prototype: An image or link to your AI-generated dashboard prototype.
  • 2. The Experiment Design: Your clearly defined hypothesis, method, and success metric.
  • 3. The Analysis & Judgment: Your plan for AI-assisted analysis and your final human-led decision (Persevere, Pivot, or Kill) with justification.
