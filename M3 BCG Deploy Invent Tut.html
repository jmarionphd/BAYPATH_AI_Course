What is This Framework?

It's a model developed by Boston Consulting Group (BCG) to help companies manage their portfolio of businesses. The goal is to ensure long-term health by simultaneously optimizing the present and building the future.

The Three Core Strategies

Deploy

Maximize the value of your strong, established businesses. Focus on operational excellence, scaling, and defending your competitive advantage.

Reshape

Transform businesses that have potential but are currently underperforming or facing disruption. This involves turnarounds, pivots, or strategic changes.

Invent

Create entirely new growth engines. This means building or acquiring new businesses, exploring new technologies, and entering new markets.

Strategy 1: Deploy

This is about your current cash cows and market leaders. The goal is not radical change, but disciplined execution and continuous improvement.

  • Focus: Efficiency, market share, and profitability.
  • Actions: Scale operations, optimize supply chains, refine marketing, and defend against competitors.
  • Mantra: "Run it better."

Strategy 2: Reshape

These are businesses at a crossroads. They may have a strong brand but outdated tech, or be in a market facing fundamental shifts. Bold action is required.

  • Focus: Transformation and strategic repositioning.
  • Actions: Turnaround plans, divest non-core parts, pivot business models, or forge new partnerships.
  • Mantra: "Change the game."

Strategy 3: Invent

This is about creating the future. It requires a different mindset—one that embraces experimentation, accepts failure as part of learning, and is separate from the core business's constraints.

  • Focus: Discovery of new business models.
  • Actions: Corporate venturing, R&D labs, strategic acquisitions, and building new businesses from scratch.
  • Mantra: "Create the new."

Balancing the Portfolio

A healthy company doesn't just choose one strategy. It actively manages a portfolio across all three categories to ensure resilience and long-term, sustainable growth.

  • Deploy: Secure and optimize today's profits.
  • Reshape: Adapt and transform for tomorrow's challenges.
  • Invent: Build the businesses of the day after tomorrow.
