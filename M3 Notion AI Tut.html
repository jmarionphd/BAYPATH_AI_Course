What is Notion AI?

Notion AI is a suite of features integrated directly into the Notion workspace. It acts as a thought partner, helping you write faster, think bigger, and augment your creativity right where your work happens.

Key Features for Documentation

Q&A on Your Docs

Instantly get answers from across your entire workspace. Ask "What is our policy on X?" and get a synthesized answer with sources.

Summarize Anything

Instantly generate concise summaries of long documents, meeting notes, or research pages.

Generate First Drafts

Create outlines, tables, how-to guides, or explain complex topics from a simple prompt to overcome writer's block.

Cost and Setup

Setup: It's an Add-On

Notion AI is not a separate product. It's an optional add-on that you can enable for your existing Notion workspace. Any workspace admin can add it from the "Settings & Members" menu.

Requirement:

You must have a paid Notion plan (Plus, Business, or Enterprise) to purchase the Notion AI add-on.

Pricing Model

The cost is calculated on a per-member, per-month basis.
