What is Stage-Gate?

Stage-Gate is a project management framework that breaks down large, complex projects into smaller phases, or Stages. Each stage is followed by a decision point, or a Gate, where key stakeholders decide if the project should continue.

The Two Core Components

Stages

These are where the work happens. Cross-functional teams perform tasks, gather information, and develop the project deliverables for the next gate.

Gates

These are the decision points. At each gate, managers review the deliverables against set criteria and make one of four decisions: Go, Kill, Hold, or Recycle.

INPUTS

Deliverables from previous stage

THE GATE MEETING

OUTPUTS

Go / Kill / Hold / Recycle Decision

Anatomy of a Gate

Gates are not just status reports; they are structured decision meetings. They ensure projects are still strategically aligned and commercially viable before more resources are invested.

  • Criteria: Are must-meet hurdles (e.g., legal, safety) and should-meet scoring criteria (e.g., market attractiveness) passed?
  • Decision: Is a clear Go/Kill decision made?

Key Benefits

Improved Success Rates

Weeds out bad projects early, focusing resources on winners.

Faster Time-to-Market

Reduces rework and ensures clear direction at every phase.

Better Resource Allocation

Ensures money and people are invested in strategically-aligned projects.

Clear Communication

Creates a shared understanding of project status and requirements.

Key Takeaways

Stage-Gate is a powerful roadmap for navigating the complexities of innovation.

  • Divide & Conquer: Break large projects into manageable stages.
  • Make Smart Decisions: Use gates to evaluate and kill weak projects early.
  • Focus Resources: Allocate your best people and funds to the best projects.
