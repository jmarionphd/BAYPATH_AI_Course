Final Assignment Goal & Components

The final assignment is the culmination of the course: you will develop a comprehensive, actionable dual transformation plan for your real-world organization (or a selected case study).

  • Design a Dual Transformation Plan: Define the core and the new.
  • Integrate Innovation Frameworks & AI Strategy: Detail the "how."
  • Identify Synergies: Connect the dots between the old and new.
  • Address Change Management: Plan for the human element of change.
  • Create an Implementation Roadmap: Chart the course for execution.
  • Final Deliverables: 3,500-Word Plan + Presentation Deck

1. Design the Dual Transformation Plan

This is the foundational concept of your entire project. You must define the two parallel paths of transformation for your organization.

Transformation A: The Core

How will you reposition the existing core business to maximize its resilience and relevance? This is about innovation *in* the core, not just efficiency.

Transformation B: The New

How will you create a new, separate business model to address future growth opportunities and disruptions? This is about building the future engine of the company.

Deliverable Component: The introduction and core thesis of your 3,500-word plan. It must clearly articulate the specific goals and nature of Transformation A and B for your chosen organization.

2. Integrate Innovation Frameworks & AI Strategy

This section details the methodologies and technologies that will power both transformations.

Elaboration:

  • Innovation Frameworks: Select and justify specific frameworks (e.g., Lean Startup, Design Thinking, Blue Ocean Strategy). Explain how they will be used, particularly to discover and validate the new business model (Transformation B).
  • AI Strategy: Detail how AI will be leveraged. This is a dual strategy in itself:
    • AI for Transformation A: Focus on optimization, automation, enhanced analytics, and customer experience in the core business.
    • AI for Transformation B: Focus on creating new AI-native products, services, or platforms that underpin the new business model.

Deliverable Component: Two dedicated, in-depth sections within your written plan. One for the chosen innovation frameworks and one for the comprehensive AI strategy, linking specific initiatives to A or B.

3. Show Integration Points and Synergies

A successful dual transformation isn't two separate companies; it's a symbiotic system. You must show how the two transformations will support and enhance each other.

Key Questions to Answer:

  • How will unique assets from the core business (data, customer relationships, brand) provide an unfair advantage to the new venture?
  • How will capabilities or technologies developed in the new venture (e.g., a new AI platform) be leveraged back to strengthen the core business?
  • How does your AI strategy directly enable your innovation process (e.g., using generative AI for ideation, analytics for market testing)?

Deliverable Component: A dedicated section, ideally supported by a diagram, that visually and narratively maps these critical integration points. This demonstrates strategic depth.

4. Address Change Management

Transformation is fundamentally about people. A brilliant strategy will fail without a plan to manage the cultural and organizational shift.

Your plan must address:

Leadership & Vision: How will leaders champion the change? How will the vision be communicated?
Cultural Shift: How will you foster a culture that can manage both the stability of the core and the experimental nature of the new?
Skills & Training: What reskilling or upskilling is needed, especially for AI adoption?
Incentives & Metrics: How will you measure and reward behaviors that support both transformations?

Deliverable Component: A robust change management section in your plan. Consider using a recognized model (e.g., Kotter's 8-Step Process) to structure your approach.

5. Create an Implementation Roadmap

This translates your strategy into a tangible, time-bound action plan. It should outline the sequence of key initiatives over a 3-5 year horizon.

Roadmap Components:

  • Phases: Break the plan into logical phases (e.g., Year 1: Foundation & Pilots, Year 2: Scaling, etc.).
  • Key Initiatives: List the major projects for both Transformation A and B in each phase.
  • Milestones & KPIs: Define clear, measurable goals for each initiative.
  • Dependencies: Show how certain initiatives depend on others.

Deliverable Component: A visual roadmap (e.g., a high-level Gantt chart or timeline graphic) included in both the written plan and the presentation, supported by detailed descriptions in the text.

Final Deliverables: The Complete Package

Your entire project culminates in two professional-grade documents that showcase your strategic thinking.

3,500-Word Strategic Plan

The comprehensive, detailed document. This is where you provide the in-depth analysis, justification, frameworks, and detailed roadmaps. It should be a formal, well-researched business document.

+

Executive Presentation Deck

A concise, persuasive summary of your plan. This is designed for a leadership audience. It should highlight the core strategy, the roadmap, and the expected business impact, compelling them to approve your vision.
